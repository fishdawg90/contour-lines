How to play

  1. Check demand. Tap a town to see what it needs, or an industry to see what it produces.
  2. Plan a route. Choose a road or rail route from New route. Connect two towns for passengers, or an industry to a buyer for its cargo. Choose Build road only to lay a road for future services.
  3. Build the line. Check the highlighted houses and streets while placing each station. Use Elevation profile to adjust the alignment before building. Choose a through station to extend the railway later. Use Add platform to give another service its own stopping track.
  4. Manage your fleet. Open a route to check deliveries and profit. For rail, choose Add train or Edit consist. Tap a model to add it, or select a vehicle in the train to move or remove it.
  5. Clear the map. Choose Bulldoze, tap an object, review the highlighted section and affected services, then confirm.
  6. Design a crossing. On the alignment map, select a marked road–rail crossing to keep it at level, span it with a bridge or take the new line underneath in a tunnel. Fast deliveries earn a bonus shown on the trip receipt.
  7. Reach more homes. Add a local bus or delivery loop to connect streets around a station.

Drag to pan and pinch to zoom. Tap the speed control to pause or speed up. Progress saves in this browser.
